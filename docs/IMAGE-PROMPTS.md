# HELIO original image prompts

Tool: built-in image_gen, two independent generation calls.

## Hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: full bleed hero photograph for HELIO, a premium solar installation website in Mexico City
Primary request: wide cinematic architectural photograph of a contemporary Mexican brutalist house made of warm concrete, its low flat rooftop covered with elegant black photovoltaic panels.
Scene/backdrop: olive trees in a lush courtyard, distant soft Mexico City mountains.
Style/medium: architectural magazine photography, highly realistic fine material detail.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9 composition. Architecture prominently in center/right and lower half. Broad negative space of dark atmospheric sky in the upper left reserved for white website title that will be overlaid separately.
Lighting/mood: warm low sunlight, quiet luxurious cinematic mood.
Color palette: moody forest green shadows, warm natural concrete, black photovoltaic panels.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no people. Black solar panels must be conspicuous, physically plausible and clearly distinguishable from the roof.

## Product detail

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: premium solar technology editorial image for the HELIO website
Primary request: macro product photograph of a black photovoltaic panel with sharp solar cells and a crisp metallic edge, crossing the composition in a dramatic diagonal.
Scene/backdrop: dark emerald studio background.
Style/medium: highly realistic luxury technology editorial photography, exquisite glass and metal material detail.
Composition/framing: close-up square composition, solar panel fills most of frame with visible edge against rich dark background.
Lighting/mood: dramatic warm sun rim lighting outlining the edge, restrained glossy highlights.
Color palette: black panel, dark emerald backdrop, warm golden rim light.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no people. Solar cells should have physically plausible regular grid lines and finely textured dark silicon surfaces.
